# Cross-border data compliance

Personal data rules for two regions:

- [Mainland China (CHN)](./CHN.md): rules when personal information leaves
  mainland China.
- [Hong Kong (HKG)](./HKG.md): rules when personal information leaves Hong
  Kong.

Each file describes **outbound** rules. An inbound-specific regime is included
only where the destination region has one; neither mainland China nor Hong Kong
has an inbound-specific transfer regime in these materials.

This applies to Company Alpha in mainland China and Company Beta in Hong Kong,
including managers and staff who access data across the border.

This is not legal advice. Confirm the final position with PRC and Hong Kong
counsel.
